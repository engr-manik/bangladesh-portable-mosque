WHO WE ARE

A mosque that comes to you.

Bangladesh Portable Mosque is an independent social initiative envisioned to make prayer facilities more accessible in busy public spaces, travel routes and underserved communities. Our journey begins in Chattogram.

MOBILE MOSQUE
CONCEPT DESIGN
DESIGNED FOR THE COMMUNITY

A thoughtful space,
on the move.

Our proposed medium-sized minibus concept is planned for 15–20 people, with practical facilities that can serve communities, visitors and people on the road.

  • Prayer area and wudu facilities
  • Private space for women
  • Maktab / education corner
  • Solar power support (planned)

Illustrative concept only. Final vehicle design, approvals and services are subject to feasibility, safety and regulatory review.

OUR FIRST STEPS

Rooted in Chattogram.
Ready to reach further.

We aim to begin in Chattogram and explore service opportunities in Cox’s Bazar — supporting worshippers, travellers, tourists and children who need access to learning.
